    Questions people ask first

    Is the dividend check really free?

    Yes. Searching costs nothing and doesn't commit you to anything. You only pay if you choose to recover, and then only 10% of what actually lands in your bank account.

    Why do you need my BVN?

    Registrars now match claims against BVN and NIN records. We use yours to prove the shares are yours and to make sure the money is paid to an account in your name. We don't use it for anything else.

    My dividends are over six years old. Are they gone?

    No. Dividends unclaimed for six years or more move to the Federal Government's Unclaimed Funds Trust Fund, where the government holds them as custodian. They can still be claimed, and we handle that claim too.

    Can I claim for my late father or mother?

    Yes, if you are an administrator or executor of the estate. You'll need the death certificate and letters of administration or probate. We tell you exactly what's missing before anything is submitted.

    Who holds my shares if I invest through Vendii?

    The Central Securities Clearing System, in your own name, under your CHN. Trades go through our sponsoring broker, Trust Bank Securities. Vendii never owns your shares.

    What happens to my shares if I borrow against them?

    They stay yours and keep earning dividends. A lien is placed on them at CSCS while the loan is open, so they can't be sold or moved. The lien lifts automatically when you repay. If the shares fall a long way, you'll be warned several times before any are sold.

    Be first in when investing and borrowing open

    Recovery is open today. Investing opens in November 2026, borrowing in March 2027. Early members get their first three months of trades without Vendii's commission.
